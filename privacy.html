Gordo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

← Help & FAQ

In short: Gordo keeps your business records on your own device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. We do not collect, see, transmit or sell your business data.

1. Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Gordo mobile application for iPhone, iPad and Android ("the App"), provided by AirraidersAnonymous ("we", "us"). You can contact us at usrnamedeleted@proton.me.

2. Information you enter into the App

The App lets you record details about your own business, which may include:

  • Quantities and amounts for deliveries you receive
  • Prices, payments and balances relating to your supplier
  • Sales you make, including cash amounts and credit terms
  • Optional customer names, references and repayment dates
  • Your own price, package size and preference settings

This information is created and stored by you, on your device, in the App's private local storage. It is not sent to us and we have no way to access it.

3. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Account credentials, because there are no accounts
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, calendar, microphone or camera content
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data
  • Analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or diagnostics sent to us
  • Your business records, which never leave your device

The App contains no third-party advertising networks, no analytics SDKs and no tracking technologies.

4. Purchases

If you obtain the App through the Apple App Store or Google Play, the store handles the transaction. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The store's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

5. Device backups

If you have enabled device backups with Apple iCloud, Google or your computer, your device's backup may include the App's local data, as it does for other apps. Those backups are controlled by you and by your backup provider, not by us.

6. Your control over your data

You can delete individual records inside the App at any time. Deleting the App from your device removes its locally stored records from that device. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to send us to exercise that right.

7. Children

The App is a business record-keeping tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Security

Your records are protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID or fingerprint. Because data is not transmitted to a server, there is no online copy of it to be breached. Keep your device passcode enabled and keep your device software up to date.

9. International users

Because the App does not transmit your records to us or to any third party, there is no international transfer of your business data by us. Any local storage and any device backup remain under your control.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App adds a feature that sends data off your device, such as cloud sync, we will update this policy and state the change in the App's release notes before that feature is enabled. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the policy last changed.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: usrnamedeleted@proton.me.
