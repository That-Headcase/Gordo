Getting started

How do I set up my prices?

Open Settings and enter your product name, your package sizes and your sale price for each size. Gordo uses those prices to work out cost, profit and margin, and to notice when a sale is above or below your price.

How does it know my cost per unit?

From the deliveries you record. Enter the quantity received and the amount your wholesaler charged you, and Gordo calculates your cost. By default it uses a weighted average of the stock you actually have on hand, so a price change from your supplier shows up in your margins straight away.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Gordo works offline. Your records are stored on your device.

Deliveries and stock

Why doesn't my stock match what's on the shelf?

Stock is calculated from your records: everything received, minus everything sold. If the number looks wrong, check for a delivery or a sale you haven't entered yet, or one entered twice. You can open any delivery to see how much of it is still unsold.

Can I correct a delivery I entered wrong?

Yes — open the delivery and edit it. Gordo recalculates your stock, your cost per unit and your supplier balance afterwards. It will warn you before a change alters past figures.

Can I record a delivery I received earlier?

Yes. The date and time are filled in automatically, and you can change the date to match when the delivery actually arrived.

Sales and credit

What happens when I sell on credit?

The stock comes off straight away, even though the money hasn't arrived yet. The sale appears in your credit book with the amount agreed and the date the customer promised to pay, and it is marked late once that date passes.

What if a customer pays only part of what they owe?

Open the credit sale and record a partial payment. The remainder stays outstanding until it's paid in full.

Do payments to my wholesaler affect my sales totals?

No. Money you pay your wholesaler and cash you receive from customers are tracked separately, so each balance stays accurate.

Gordo himself

Why is he shouting at me?

He reacts to your numbers. Sell at your price and he's pleased. Sell below it and he'll ask why you're buckling. Give credit and he gets twitchy, because he'd rather you got paid.

How do I turn him off?

Settings has a switch for Gordo, a reduced-motion option that holds him still, and a setting for his swearing symbols.

Does he ever block me from saving something?

Never. He reacts after your record is saved. His opinion has no effect on your figures.

Your data

Where are my records kept?

On your device, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account to sign into. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

If you have device backups enabled (iCloud, Google or a computer), your phone's normal restore process may carry the app's data across. Before relying on that, keep your own copy of your figures by other means until you've confirmed the records arrived intact.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app removes the records it holds on that device. Export or back up first if you need to keep them.
